Exam facts (CAT, English)

  • 100–150 questions, 3 hours, adaptive (CAT); linear form elsewhere: 250 questions / 6 hours.
  • Pass mark: 700 / 1000 scaled. No penalty for wrong answers — never leave blanks.
  • Experience requirement: 5 years cumulative paid work in 2+ domains (or 4 years + a degree/approved credential = 1-year waiver). You can pass FIRST and get endorsed within 9 months.
  • Question style: scenario → “BEST” answer. Think risk, cost, and people/process before tooling. When two answers look right, choose the one a senior security advisor would recommend to management.
