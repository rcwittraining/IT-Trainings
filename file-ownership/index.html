What you will practise

  1. Change owner — Change owner to www-data with chown. 34 pts
  2. Change group — Change group with chgrp. 33 pts
  3. Both at once — Use chown user:group. 33 pts

Commands used in this lab

  • chown www-data /data/web/index.html
