About RCW IT Training

RCW IT Training is an independent educational platform for enterprise infrastructure skills. It is built and maintained by a working infrastructure practitioner who has spent years operating VMware platforms, Linux estates, backup and storage systems, Active Directory environments and cloud workloads in production.

The idea behind the site is simple: most people learn infrastructure best by doing. So every part of the platform is built around hands-on practice rather than passive reading.

What you will find here

  • Free simulators and labs — interactive ESXi, Kubernetes, Jenkins, Windows Server and Active Directory labs that run in your browser, with no account required.
  • Troubleshooting guides — long-form articles on real production problems: Active Directory replication, Kerberos and DFS, backup failures, storage performance, vSphere upgrade planning and more, each written from operational experience.
  • Scenario challenges and certificates — timed diagnostic scenarios and self-assessment quizzes for certification readiness.
  • Security updates — a weekly index of official Microsoft security advisories, collected to help teams plan patching.
  • Downloadable tools — small utilities for lab and operations work.

How the content is made

The guides and labs on this site are produced from real operational scenarios — issues that come up in production infrastructure work, tested and verified in lab environments before they are published. Articles are reviewed and updated as products and best practices change.
