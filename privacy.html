Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026
RCW IT Training does not require a learner account. Interactive lab inputs and results stay in the learner's browser. Selected informational pages use Google AdSense, which may process device and usage information as described below.

1. Scope and site operator

This policy applies to www.rcwittraining.in, operated by RCW IT Training in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. It explains data handling on the website, including advertising and consent choices. External services linked from this website operate under their own privacy policies.

Privacy contact: info@rcwittraining.in.

2. Training labs and information you provide

No account or registration is required to use the labs. Lab commands, answers, scores, names entered for certificates, and generated certificate/report data are processed locally in the browser unless a page clearly says otherwise. Downloaded files are created on the learner's device.

Some labs use browser storage to preserve progress or preferences. Learners can remove that information using their browser's site-data controls. The catalogue search runs locally and search terms are not submitted to RCW IT Training.

3. Hosting and technical records

The site is delivered through a website hosting provider. As with most hosting services, technical request records may include an IP address, browser or device type, requested URL, date and time, referrer, and security-related events. The hosting provider controls its own operational logs and retention practices.

4. Google AdSense advertising

Selected informational pages use Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, may place or read cookies, use web beacons, process IP addresses or other identifiers, and collect information about ad impressions and interactions to serve, limit, secure, personalize, and measure advertising.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google's Privacy Policy, and Google advertising technologies.

RCW IT Training does not send names, email addresses, certificate names, lab answers, or other directly identifying learner-entered information to Google for advertising.

5. Consent, cookies, and privacy choices

RCW IT Training uses Google's certified consent management platform for applicable advertising messages. Depending on location, visitors can consent, decline, or manage individual purposes and advertising partners. Consent Mode communicates applicable choices for advertising storage, ad personalization, and ad user data to supported Google services.

Where consent is legally required, it is requested for non-essential storage and personalized advertising. If consent is declined, Google may restrict ad processing or show limited ads where permitted. The site does not currently use Google Analytics.

Privacy and cookie settings

Visitors can also manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Browser controls can delete or block cookies, although doing so may affect saved preferences.

Google may also add a consent-revocation link automatically at the bottom of approved pages where its European regulations message and AdSense code are active.

6. Other regions and opt-out rights

Where regional law provides a right to opt out of targeted advertising, sale, or sharing as those terms are legally defined, the applicable Google privacy message provides available choices. Requests about RCW IT Training's own handling of information can be sent to the privacy contact above.

7. Data sharing, retention, and international processing

Advertising and hosting providers process information for the purposes described in this policy and under their own terms. They may process information in countries other than the visitor's country. Their retention periods depend on the service, account settings, security needs, and legal requirements. RCW IT Training does not sell learner registration data because the site does not operate learner accounts.

8. Children

The site provides general IT training and is not directed to children under 13. Visitors who are not old enough to make privacy choices under their local law should use the site with a parent or guardian.

9. Security, rights, and policy changes

Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce unauthorized access and keep interactive lab data local. No Internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Depending on applicable law, visitors may have rights to ask about, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of their personal information. Contact info@rcwittraining.in with a specific request. Identity may need to be verified before a request is completed.

This policy may be updated when services or legal requirements change. The revision date at the top identifies the current version.
