Enterprise Windows Autopilot & Compliance Architecture

Windows Autopilot enables IT departments to deliver a modern zero-touch deployment experience: new devices are shipped directly from OEM vendors to end-users without requiring IT imaging.

Compliance Standard Checklist (Zero-Trust Standard)

  • Device Encryption: BitLocker must be enabled with XTS-AES 256-bit encryption with key escrow to Entra ID.
  • Hardware Security: TPM 2.0 chip and UEFI Secure Boot must be enabled and attested.
  • OS Baseline: Minimum OS Build 10.0.22631 (Windows 11 23H2) to prevent known kernel vulnerabilities.
  • Endpoint Protection: Microsoft Defender Antivirus Real-time Protection and Cloud-delivered protection enabled.
