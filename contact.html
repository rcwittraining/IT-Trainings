Certificates and scorecards

Can you re-issue my certificate?
No — certificates are generated in your browser and nothing is stored on a server, so there is nothing to look up. Re-run the lab; it takes a few minutes and the new certificate is just as valid.
Are RCW certificates recognised by Red Hat, Microsoft or Cisco?
No. They are practice-completion certificates from an independent site. They show that you did the work; they are not vendor credentials.
Can I list them on LinkedIn?
Yes, under Licenses & Certifications with "RCW IT Training" as the issuer, or as a post with your scorecard.
