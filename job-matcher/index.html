CAREER TOOL · RUNS IN YOUR BROWSER

Job Match & Apply Kit — every listing, scored against your resume

Point it at your CV, set the roles you actually want, and it pulls live listings from public job APIs, ranks them by real skill overlap, then writes the application packet for each match: tailored cover letter, portal answers, and a field-by-field sheet you can paste into any application form.

What it does, automatically

  • Searches several public sources at once, de-duplicates, re-scores on every filter change
  • Explains every score: which requirement matched, which is missing, what to do about it
  • Generates a cover letter per job from evidence found in your resume
  • Batch-prepares packets for every job above your threshold — the part that takes the hours
  • Tracks the pipeline and exports it to CSV for your own spreadsheet

What it deliberately does not do

  • It never logs into Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed or any portal, and never submits on your behalf. Those platforms prohibit automation in their terms, accounts get restricted for it, and an unreviewed application is the fastest way to be filtered out at the screening call.
  • It never invents employers, dates, degrees or certifications. Anything your resume does not say arrives as a [FILL] marker you have to answer.
  • No server, no upload, no login, no analytics: parsing, scoring and storage all happen in this tab.

      6 · Sources, keys and privacy

      Which APIs this tab calls, what it needs from you, and the rules it will not break.

      Job sources

      Only public APIs that permit browser calls are listed. Sources without a key are free to use; the two aggregate search APIs need a free key of your own, kept in this browser and sent only to that API.

      Company career boards (Greenhouse / Lever / SmartRecruiters)

      These are the employers’ own official APIs — the cleanest listings you can get, straight from the ATS, with a real apply link. Add the board slug for a company, one per line.

      Slug = the word in boards.greenhouse.io/<slug>. Greenhouse asks that you link back to the posting, which the tool does by keeping the company’s own URL on every card.

      Optional API keys

      Never required. Stored in localStorage on this device only, sent to the named API alone, and never logged anywhere by this site — this site has no server.

      If a key-based source fails with a CORS error, that provider does not allow direct browser calls — the source list will say so, and nothing else breaks.

      Refresh etiquette

      How this tool behaves, in writing

      • No credential handling. There is no login form, no cookie set by us, and nothing that accepts a password. If a tool asks for your Naukri or LinkedIn password, that is a scam — including if the tool looks like this one.
      • No automated submission. Filling and sending forms on job portals breaks their terms, gets accounts restricted, and — when a template is wrong — puts a fabricated application in front of an employer. The tool prepares; you press send.
      • No scraping of login-walled sites. Only documented public APIs, with their stated etiquette: Remotive asks for attribution and a few calls a day, so the tool caches, labels each listing with its source and rate limits itself.
      • Local-only storage. Profile, targets, listings cache, packets and tracker live in your browser under rcwjm.v1.*. “Delete everything” is the button above. No copy goes to this site, because there is no backend to receive it.
      • Advertising and consent. This tool page carries no ad units. Site-wide policy, including how to change consent choices, is in the privacy policy.
      • Honesty beats hit rate. The generator marks what it does not know instead of guessing. A match you cannot defend in an interview costs you the interview and the employer’s trust in your other applications.
